# Lyme Property Tax Card Explainer

Lyme Property Tax records are now recorded
in Avitar Associates's database
(replacing the earlier Vision database). 
Here's a quick intro to the new website:

1. Go to the Avitar Associates website [https://data.avitarassociates.com/logon.aspx](https://data.avitarassociates.com/logon.aspx) 
2. Click **GUEST**, then type the letters into the _Challenge Phrase_ box. 
3. Click **Lyme** in the list of towns
4. You will see an empty screen. Don't be daunted
5. At the upper-left, click **Searches** and choose **By address...**
6. Click the **small square** to the left of a **road name**.
7. You will see a list of house numbers. Click the **small square** to the left of a **house number**.
8. Finally, you will see the "Tax Card" information. The **General** tab shows:
  - **Parcel ID** (Map/Lot/Unit)
  - **Owners and location**
  - **Acres**
  - **Valuation** for the building, features, land, and total "Parcel Value"
  - **Notes** giving a very dense description of the
    features of the property in "assessor-speak".
    Copy/paste that text into the box below and click
    **Explain** to get a human-readable interpretation.

- Only the **General** tab contains information.
  Avitar requires a license to see the data in other tabs:
  Lyme has chosen not to purchase a license for town-wide
